Stage 1 is parallel — Planning and QA review simultaneously. The document only advances to Stage 2 once both approve. Stage 2 (Project Manager) and Stage 3 (Management) run sequentially. Management's approval requires a typed digital signature and immediately publishes the document. A rejection at any stage returns the document to the uploader, who can submit a new revision that restarts the chain from Stage 1.
